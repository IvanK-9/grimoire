# R-013: Show-to-player screens

**Phase:** Later · **Priority:** Could · **Status:** Draft

## User story
As the Storyteller, I want full-screen cards I can show to a player so that I can give information silently at night.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A "You are" card with the character's artwork and name.
- [ ] Standard info cards: "This is the Demon", "These are your Minions", "These characters are not in play", and custom text.
- [ ] Nothing from the grimoire is visible while a card is shown.
