# 0001: Record decisions as ADRs

**Status:** Proposed · **Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Decided by:** Product owner / architect

## Context
Several people will refine requirements and the architecture over time. The reasons behind choices need to survive changes in who is working on the project.

## Decision
Record every significant product or technical decision as an Architecture Decision Record in `docs/decisions/`. Once accepted, an ADR is not edited. A change of mind means writing a new ADR that supersedes the old one.

## Consequences
- The history of decisions, and the reasons for them, stays readable.
- Writing an ADR adds a small cost to each decision.
